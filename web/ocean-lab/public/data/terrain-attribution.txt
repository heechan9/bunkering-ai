Natural Earth 1:10m land boundaries: public domain.

Processing: Terrarium PNG decoded to metres, bilinearly resampled onto a 257 × 257 WGS84 display grid per region, rounded to whole metres. Original source tile URLs and SHA-256 hashes accompany each regional JSON. Native source resolution varies; this is not survey-quality terrain. Acquisition: 2026-09-12.

Rendering: land mask from simplified Natural Earth polygons; negative elevations clamped to zero for display; no bathymetry. Horizontal equirectangular approximation at regional centre latitude. Height scale selectable 1×, 4×, 8×, 12×, default 8×. Colour indicates elevation, not vegetation or satellite imagery. Reduced mesh detail on compatible rendering mode. Terrain is a geographic exhibit only, not connected to experiment inputs, rewards, vessel physics or routing. Not for navigation. No endorsement by data providers is implied.
